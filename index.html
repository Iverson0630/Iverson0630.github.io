Understanding movement.
Learning to assist.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning with human musculoskeletal models and exoskeleton control. I study the intersection of human movement, simulation, and wearable robotics.

I am a member of the Biomechatronics and Intelligent Robotics Lab (BIROLAB), advised by Prof. Hao Su. I received my bachelor's and master's degrees in automation from Tianjin University.

From human movement

01

Model

Human musculoskeletal
modeling

Studying movement through models of muscles, joints, and skeletal mechanics, with a focus on their role in learning and control.

How can a simulated human provide a useful foundation for learning movement?

02

Learn

Reinforcement learning
for movement

Investigating reinforcement learning for the control of human musculoskeletal models and the study of movement in simulation.

How can control policies learn to coordinate a musculoskeletal system?

03

Assist

Exoskeleton
control

Exploring control strategies for wearable robots, with an emphasis on the interaction between human movement and robotic assistance.
